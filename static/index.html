🖥 ScreenShare — control room

Create a session, then send the share link to the person whose screen you need to see and control. Open the watch link on your computer to see their screen live.

How control works

  1. They open the share link and tick the consent box (view and control).
  2. They download agent.py from the share page and run it on that computer: pip install pyautogui websockets then python agent.py <room>.
  3. Once they consent, your mouse / keyboard / scroll on the watch page controls their screen.

Nothing happens until they consent — the agent refuses to act before that.
